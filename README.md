# 📊 Multi-Channel Marketing Performance Analysis & Sales Prediction

## 📌 Project Overview

In today's competitive digital retail environment, businesses invest heavily in multiple marketing channels such as Social Media, Search, Email, Affiliate, Influencer, and TV advertising.

However, businesses often struggle to understand which marketing strategies generate higher revenue and how factors such as advertising spend, pricing, discounts, market reach, and customer segments influence sales.

This project provides an end-to-end **Marketing Analytics and Sales Prediction System** that combines:

- 📊 Exploratory Data Analysis
- 🤖 Machine Learning
- 📈 Marketing Performance Analysis
- 🎯 Sales Revenue Prediction
- 🌐 Interactive Streamlit Application

The system analyzes marketing campaign data and predicts the expected **Sales Revenue** using a **K-Nearest Neighbors (KNN) Regression** model.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

The major objectives of this project are:

- Predict sales revenue using marketing and campaign-related features.
- Analyze the performance of different advertising channels.
- Understand the influence of advertising spend on revenue.
- Analyze the impact of pricing and discount strategies.
- Identify high-performing customer segments.
- Analyze regional marketing performance.
- Provide an interactive prediction interface using Streamlit.
- Support data-driven marketing and budget allocation decisions.

---

## 💼 Business Problem

Businesses invest significant amounts of money in digital advertising but may not know which campaigns and channels generate the best revenue.

Traditional approaches often focus on individual metrics such as:

- Impressions
- Click-through rate
- Advertising spend

However, campaign performance is influenced by multiple factors simultaneously, including:

- Product price
- Discount rate
- Market reach
- Customer segment
- Marketing channel
- Region
- Impressions
- Campaign characteristics

Therefore, a holistic data-driven approach is required to analyze campaign performance and predict potential revenue.
